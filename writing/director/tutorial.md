# Director: room tutorial (draft for Chip to edit)

The room slice after the cold open. The director walks the assistant through one take, then
counts them in. Draft written 2026-11-13 from Chip's outline; **edit freely**. Once it's settled
the lines are voiced (same fal voice as the cold open, Orus, or Chip's own recording) and
played in this order by `tutorial.verse`.

Conventions follow `writing/README.md`: text in backticks is the line as spoken, the id is the
stable key (and the audio file name: `VO_Director_<id>.wav`), notes outside backticks are for us.
Lines 4 and 5 reuse the writing stream's `dir_d1_intro` and `dir_d1_file` (from
`writing/des/dream-1.md`), lightly joined.

## Lines

### tut_01_again

When: the room comes up after DEAD AIR boots.

`Alright. We'll go over it one more time.`

### tut_02_job

`Our job is to get inside their head. Find what's got them stuck, and help them let go of it.`

### tut_03_movie

`Think of it like a movie. They're stuck in one scene, playing it over and over. We give it an ending.`

### tut_04_patient

When: the light finds the recliner, or the case file's header shows. Same as `dir_d1_intro`.

`Dessen Varro. Thirteen. Second-hand speech implant, no warranty. Same dream every night, and it ends the same way.`

### tut_05_file

Then the game waits for the player to open the case file on the computer (one key; which one
is decided with the screen).

`Open his file on the computer. Read all of it. The software flags what it can't explain. Those are the good parts.`

### tut_06_setup

When: the player closes the case file. Then the directing menu opens: casting, scene settings,
items.

`Now set up the take. Who you'll be in his dream, how the scene looks, what you bring in. Details from his life reach him. Comfort doesn't.`

### tut_07_countdown

When: the player confirms the setup. Then the projector sound (`SFX_Dream_Transition`), the
house lights go down (vignette overlay), and a light comes up on Des (`T_FX_Spot_Recliner`).

`Quiet on set. Three. Two. One.`
