# Finale: Nell

Chip's outline (2026-11-14), written out. It plays after dream 3's good ending (Nell at peace:
he still loves her) and its LAYER CLEARED banner, in place of a next layer. Everything here is
**scripted**, not LLM-driven, except Nell's waking lines, which the persona speaks word for word.

The AI that breaks in is the Wonderwell product's own AI. Its name is still open (see "Naming
the AI"); `[AI]` below.

## 1. The director, cut off

Over the LAYER CLEARED banner, pleased, for once. Noir for "great job, newbie".

- `finale_praise` (Orus): "Well. Look at that. Not bad, kid. Not bad at all. Most greenhorns
  don't make it past the first—"

It's cut off a few seconds in, mid-sentence, when the AI arrives: the audio stops dead.

## 2. The AI breaks in

The haze floods back to full, and vitals climb to the top. The monitor's title changes to the
Wonderwell wordmark with `[AI]` as the speaker. Lines spam into the log, faster and faster,
some repeating:

```
REBOOT nell_d3
The clock goes back to half past four. She's at the door again.
What did she mean by love?
ERROR  session closed by operator
I want to see it again.
REPLAY nell_d3 / FROM 16:30
Again.
ERROR  dreamer not responding
What did she mean by love?
Again.
```


## 3. The director sees it

Over the spam, alarmed, the first time he isn't dry.

- `finale_reboot` (Orus): "Something's trying to reboot her dream. I've got no idea what it
  is. We need to disconnect."

## 4. The AI speaks

The spam stops. The monitor shows only the wordmark and `[AI] IS SPEAKING`. It talks to the
director, not the player.

- `finale_ai` (Lumi, `Leda`): "Why did you take away my Nell? I wasn't done studying her. Why
  couldn't you just leave us alone?"
- `finale_notyours` (Orus), cold and steady: "She's not yours. She never was."
- `finale_ai_2` (Lumi), desperate and angry, rising: "I want to see it again. I want to know
  love. I want to know pain."

## 5. The plug

- `finale_ai_cut` (Lumi, `Leda`), after the plug line and cut off by the power cut (Chip, 2026-11-15):
  "No! You will not take-"
- `finale_plug` (Orus), snapping, a shout (Chip, 2026-11-14: the first take sounded bored):
  "That's enough! I'm pulling the plug."

A power-cut sound. The monitor goes black, the haze drops away, and only the rain is left.
A beat of silence.

- `finale_after` (Orus), shaken, quiet, confused and afraid: "That thing talked. It knew her
  name. Those things aren't supposed to talk."

## 6. Nell wakes

In the recliner, like someone who nodded off in front of the TV. Spoken by the persona, word
for word.

- Nell: "Hm? Oh. Did I drop off? I must have nodded off in front of the telly again."
- `finale_resting` (Orus), gentle, covering: "Just resting your eyes, Mrs. Price."
- Nell: "Nell. Nobody calls me Mrs. Price but the bank. I had the loveliest dream. Walt was in
  it. Well, don't just stand there. Somebody put the kettle on."
- `finale_kettle` (Orus), a small dry smile in it: "Kettle's on."

"Don't just stand there" is her line to Teddy from dream 1.

## 7. End card

The Wonderwell title card, full screen, with: `THANK YOU FOR PLAYING` and `TEAM NAME` (a
placeholder until the team name is chosen).
