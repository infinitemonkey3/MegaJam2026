# Case file: Eleanor "Nell" Price

Client id: `nell`. Everything under "Screen" is shown to the player, in the debug software's
record style. Everything under "Full Fact Sheet" is hidden and staged by dream. Years are left
relative on purpose ("6 yr ago"), so the game never has to name the present year.

Entries marked `ANOMALY` are the software's own flag for a record it can't explain. They are the
planted odd entries. The "Pays off" notes after an arrow are for us and are not shown.

## Full Fact Sheet

Not on the screen. This is what the software's records hold (the "ask the software" bonus) and
what the client's personality can draw on. A stage's facts become available when the dream
before it resolves. Later stages never leak into an earlier dream's prompts.

### Stage 0: Available From The Start

- `F0-01` Third loaf. W. Price, trade-paper interview, undated clipping: "Third loaf's for
  luck. Nell never ate it."
- `F0-02` Division of work. Walter: bread. Nell: the sweet side. Rolls, buns, cakes.
- `F0-03` W. Price's apron. Hangs on the hook by the oven. Not washed in 6 years. Nobody

- `F0-04` Theodore Price, 34. Left baking at 19. Works nights at the freight depot. Asked the

- `F0-06` Regulars on record. Mrs. Okafor: two seeded rolls. Dunn, night porter: heel of rye.

- `F0-08` Subject still sweeps the shop daily. Meter confirms: no oven use.

### Stage 1: Unlocked By Dream 1

- `F1-02` The March 3 cake: 8 inch, lemon. Same order every year. She bakes it, writes the

### Stage 3: Unlocked By Dream 3

- `F3-01` Walter Price died on March 3, his birthday, 6 yr ago. 16:40. Cutler Row, two

- `F3-03` They had words over it. Her last words to him: "Then you can pick it up yourself."
- `F3-04` She baked it anyway. It was in the case at 5 PM.

## Dream Reveals

The fact each dream gives up when it resolves. The client says it in her own voice (see each
dream's good ending).

### Dream 1 Reveal

March 3 was Walt's birthday. The cake is his, and nobody else gets to take it. The whistling was
his too.

### Dream 2 Reveal

Walt's whistling was the oven clock. Three times through the tune and the bread was done. The
morning of the funeral she stood at the oven waiting for a tune that didn't come.

### Dream 3 Reveal

Walt died on his birthday, on his way back to pick up the cake. He had ordered it himself. Her
last words to him were "Then you can pick it up yourself." She has been holding it for him ever
since.

## Layers

### Layer 1: The Surface Loop

5 AM at the bakery. The oven won't light, the dough won't hold, faceless customers press at the
glass, the shop is silent, and the lights go out. Written in `dream-1.md`.

### Layer 2: The Memory Under It

The morning of Walt's funeral. She opens alone. The oven works this time, too well. The bread
goes in and she waits for a signal she can't name. Everything burns. The surreal layer: clocks
without hands, Walt's apron kneading by itself, flour raining on the mourners. Written in
`dream-2.md`.

### Layer 3: The Core

March 3, six years ago. The last ordinary day. A cake in the case, an order slip in his
handwriting, and 5 PM coming. Quiet on purpose. Written in `dream-3.md`.

## Leaves Behind

When Nell is out for good: a Price's Bakery order slip for a birthday cake, never picked up. The
handwriting on it isn't hers and isn't Walt's. To write Friday, with the director's line.
